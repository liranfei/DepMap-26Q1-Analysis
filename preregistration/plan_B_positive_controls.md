## 1. Positive-control definition

The unit of judgement is a **candidate pair × source paper**. A gene–lineage pair is eligible if **at least one** source paper meets criteria 1–5 on its own (criteria are not combined across papers). Papers with conflicting results are recorded but do not remove eligibility. Criteria 1–4 must be met by experiments performed and reported in that paper; data reproduced from dependency databases or other published screens (e.g. a DepMap-based figure) never count.

The screeners judge experimental facts (criteria 1, 2 and 5, and the effect in each named cell line). Which lineage each cell line belongs to is decided by script with the lineage rule in `v1_materials/README.md` (the same rule for target and comparison lines; non-cancer models do not count), and criteria 3 and 4 are computed from the screeners' structured fields:

1. **Perturbation**: loss of function by shRNA, siRNA, CRISPR knockout/interference, dominant-negative construct or targeted degrader, performed in cancer cells of the target lineage, reduces proliferation or viability (at least one target-lineage cancer line with effect "met"). Pharmacological inhibition alone is recorded separately and does not count for the primary set.
2. **Reliability**: at least 2 independent reagents (e.g. 2 shRNAs/sgRNAs) or a rescue experiment.
3. **Breadth** (computed): at least 2 cancer cell lines assigned to the target lineage with effect "met", or 1 such line plus in vivo "met".
4. **Selectivity** (computed): at least 2 cancer cell lines assigned to a lineage other than the target lineage, each with "no effect or an effect at most half as large as in the target lines" judged "met". Non-cancer lines, and lines assigned to the target lineage, do not count.
5. **Origin of the gene (circularity rule)**: the gene was not nominated from DepMap, Project Achilles, Project DRIVE, Project Score, DEMETER2 component screens or any other genome-scale loss-of-function screen (one targeting at least 1,000 genes). Operational test: the reason for studying the gene as stated by the source paper itself, followed back one level of citation. Papers of the form "we found X in DepMap, then validated it" are excluded. A gene first nominated by a genome-scale screen is excluded even if others later confirmed it independently. Focused library screens (fewer than 1,000 genes) do not exclude a gene.
6. **Identifiers**: each source paper has a PMID or DOI, verified programmatically (PubMed/Crossref title and year match) before step 2 of screening; unverifiable sources are removed and listed in v2. Gene = current HGNC symbol with Entrez ID; lineage assigned by the rule and mapping table frozen in v1 (`v1_materials/README.md`, `lineage_mapping.csv`). Pairs whose lineage is unclear under that rule are listed but not analysed.

Judging rules: with the full text, information not reported counts as "not met" and information reported but ambiguous as "unclear". With the abstract only, items the abstract does not address are "unclear" (not assessable), not "not met". A computed condition is "met" if it holds with "met" lines only, "not met" if it fails even when all "unclear" lines are counted as "met", and "unclear" otherwise. Further rules:
- The target lineage of each unit is fixed when the units are formed (README, rules between steps) and is not re-determined after step 2; after step 2 the script assigns a lineage to each listed cell line and computes the criteria against the fixed target lineage.
- Final criterion 1 = the screener's c1 AND "at least one cancer line assigned to the target lineage with effect 'met'".
- Three-valued AND, used for criterion 1 and for "eligible" (criteria 1–5): any "not met" → "not met"; all "met" → "met"; otherwise "unclear".
- In vivo "not done" counts as "not met".
- Each cell line counts once per unit: duplicates are removed by matched ModelID, or by normalised name for unmatched lines.
- A line listed as a target line but assigned by the script to another lineage is treated as a comparison line: a target effect "met" (reduced proliferation) counts as "not met" for selectivity, otherwise "unclear". A line listed as a comparison line but assigned to the target lineage counts for neither criterion 3 nor criterion 4.
- Adjudication and full-text checks change the screeners' structured fields (c1, c2, c5 and the effect of each line), never the computed criteria directly; the script then recomputes. Field tables before and after adjudication are both kept for v2.

Recorded for each unit, not used for exclusion:
- Origin category: DepMap-derived (DepMap, Project Achilles, Project DRIVE, Project Score, DEMETER2 component screens) / other genome-scale screen / biologically motivated candidate / genomics (amplification, mutation) / clinical / unclear. Here "unclear" means not from a genome-scale screen (criterion 5 met) but fitting none of the other categories; whether the gene came from a screen at all is criterion 5. A pair whose eligible papers are all of origin "unclear" is excluded from the primary set and included in a sensitivity analysis.
- Subgroup-restricted dependency (e.g. a dependency limited to a receptor-positive or mutant subtype): included and flagged.
- In vivo evidence in the paper.
- Cell lines used, with the effect in each (most are also in DepMap; reported descriptively).
- Affiliations of the corresponding and last authors, as printed.
- Publication year.
- Whether the unit was judged on the full text or on the abstract only.

Aggregated by the adjudicator at the pair level:
- Evidence grade: A = eligible papers from ≥ 2 independent laboratories (different corresponding authors at different institutions), or an eligible paper with in vivo "met"; B = otherwise.
- Screen-project flag: an eligible paper is flagged if the printed affiliation of its corresponding or last author matches `v1_materials/screen_project_institutions.csv` (rule in README).

Sensitivity analyses that remove papers (screen-project flag, year ≤ 2016 kept only, origin "unclear" included) remove or keep **papers**, and eligibility of each pair is then re-determined by the "at least one paper on its own" rule.

## 2. Search and screening

- Search by cancer type, not by gene, with the 26 lineage queries (one for each lineage with ≥ 5 lines in DepMap 26Q1) and the review query exactly as listed in `v1_materials/search_queries.md` (authoritative). Each is sorted by Best Match and the first 100 records are taken; the search date, the hit count and the full list of PMIDs returned (all records, not only the first 100) are recorded. For reviews, candidate genes and the primary papers cited for them are taken, one level of snowballing only. Truncation at 100 is expected to matter mainly for the two blood lineages (Q15, Q16), and is evaluated afterwards with the full PMID lists.
- **Screener 1**: an AI agent run in an environment with no access to the analysis files (a cloud or web session without the local file system, or a different machine or account), so that the repository, S2 Table and manuscript cannot be read; the environment is recorded. **Screener 2**: a different AI model (not Claude), run by the author. Both receive the same prompts, frozen in v1 (`v1_materials/screener_prompt.md`), which contain no Garraway gene, no S2 pair and no gene named in this plan; neutral examples only. Model names and versions are recorded.
- **Web access and logs**: both screeners may use web access only for PubMed/PMC, DOI resolution and publishers' full-text pages, and the prompts forbid using any repository, archive, preprint or preregistration of this study. Every URL visited or tool call made in each run is logged and deposited in v2. After each run the log is checked by script for any visit to github.com, zenodo.org, osf.io or biorxiv.org and to any other host outside the allowed set; a run with a visit to a record of this study, or to a host outside the allowed set that is not a publisher's full-text page, is discarded and rerun, and this is reported. If a screener's environment cannot produce such a log, this is recorded and stated as a limitation.
- Two steps: (1) each screener extracts candidate pairs from the records; (2) after identifier verification and normalisation, the union of both lists (merge rules in README) forms a fixed list of "candidate pair × source paper" units, which both screeners judge independently. Agreement is computed on step 2 with this unit, for c1, c2, c5, the computed c3 and c4, and "eligible": raw agreement, counts, the proportion "met" for each screener, unweighted Cohen's kappa (three categories) and Gwet's AC1. For step 1 the overlap of the two candidate lists is reported.
- Adjudication by the author against the written criteria, with reasons recorded; borderline cases also go to a third reviewer (another person or model). Adjudication is completed before v2.
- Full-text checks by the author: (a) every pair that enters the primary set (criteria 1–5); (b) every unit that either screener judged on the abstract only and that is not "not met" on any criterion (including the computed criteria 1, 3 and 4) for either screener; such units that cannot be read in full are "not assessable", and their number is reported; (c) of the excluded candidates, those excluded for origin or selectivity are all re-checked if there are fewer than 50, otherwise a random 20% (seed 40261120). The proportion of "abstract only" units is reported for each screener.
- Limitations stated in advance: language models may "remember" DepMap results; this preregistration and the study repository are public, so a model may have met them in training or retrieval; the search terms favour lineage transcription factors, so the positive set is not a random sample of real lineage dependencies (for example metabolic dependencies are under-represented); 9 lineages had fewer than 10 records at the pre-freeze count checks, so positive controls will concentrate in a few lineages (the number of positive controls per lineage is reported).
- Screening log (every paper considered, decision, reason) is kept for v2.

## 3. Endpoints

Ranking universe: all testable gene–lineage pairs of the primary analysis (467,091, from the existing `all_tests.csv`). All orderings are ascending (most negative selectivity, or smallest p, first); ties get the average rank, and the size of each positive control's tie is reported. Positive controls that are not testable are listed and excluded.

Rankings:
- R0: raw p value only.
- R1 (mirrors the significance step): pairs with q < 0.05 first, ordered by selectivity (median difference); then all other pairs ordered by raw p value.
- R2 (mirrors the full rule): pairs with q < 0.05 and median < −1 first, ordered by selectivity; then the other pairs with q < 0.05, ordered by selectivity; then all other pairs ordered by raw p value.
- For a method Mx of plan A (M2 or M3), R1(Mx) has the same structure: pairs with q_Mx < 0.05 ordered by the primary effect size of Mx, then the rest by p_Mx; the universe is the pairs testable under both M1 and Mx, and for any comparison with R1(Mx), R1 is re-ranked within the same common universe (q values kept from M1); positive controls testable under only one are listed. "Top 94" and "top 376" mean the first 94 and 376 ranks, regardless of the size of the method's own prioritised set.
- Exploratory: selectivity alone, mean difference, Cohen's d, AUC, the secondary effect size of M2.

Structural fact stated in advance: the BH-significant set is always a prefix of the p-value ordering (the 9,805 pairs with q < 0.05 are exactly the 9,805 smallest p values, about the top 2.1%). R1 and R2 therefore differ from R0 only inside this block; outside it all three rankings are identical.

- **Primary endpoint**: among the N positive controls inside the q < 0.05 block, D = (1/N) Σ_g Σ_{i∈g} d_i with d_i = log10(rank under R1) − log10(rank under R0) (negative = R1 ranks them higher); the same D is used for the point estimate, the test and the bootstrap. Inference: exact sign-flip test clustered by gene (all d_i of gene g change sign together; all 2^G sign patterns if G ≤ 16 genes, otherwise 100,000 random patterns, seed 40261121), two-sided. A 95% percentile bootstrap CI clustered by gene (2,000 replicates, seed 40261122) is reported as description. Computed only if the conditions of Section 4 hold. A difference is claimed only if p < 0.05; with 15–30 eligible pairs it is called an "exploratory difference". Otherwise the result is "not distinguishable"; no claim of "similar performance". The result answers only whether, among significant pairs, ordering by selectivity or by p value places known dependencies higher; it is not written as "the rule is better".
- **Sensitivity of the primary endpoint**: (a) only pairs with at least one unit (same source paper) computed "eligible" both from screener 1's fields and from screener 2's fields, with the fixed target lineage, without adjudication or full-text checks (pairs eligible only through different papers for the two screeners are not included); (b) without subgroup-restricted pairs; (c) grade A only; (d) origin "unclear" included; (e) only papers from ≤ 2016; (f) without papers carrying the screen-project flag (pair eligibility re-determined as in Section 1).
- **Per-control table (descriptive)**: for every positive control, q value, median gene effect, selectivity, ranks under R0, R1 and R2 with tie sizes, and which criterion it fails, if any (significance, median < −1, selectivity < −0.5).
- **Stratified vs unstratified (descriptive only, no test)**: every positive control that moves into or out of the top 94 / top 376 / q < 0.05 block between R1(recommended method of plan A, Section 4.6) and R1, flagged blood or non-blood. Expectation stated in advance (not a hypothesis test): non-blood positive controls keep their position; blood positive controls may fall, because the estimand changes. Paired log10 rank differences with bootstrap CI are exploratory.
- **Other exploratory**: McClish-standardised partial AUROC up to FPR 0.001 (about the top 467 pairs) for R0, R1 and R2; recall in the top 94, 188 and 376; the exploratory rankings; recall among the 94 prioritised pairs (not blind).
- Notes for the report: AUC ranking and the Mann–Whitney statistic are equivalent up to a constant, so p-only and AUC rankings are strongly related. Selectivity alone is not a fair ranking: of the 213 tests with selectivity < −0.5, 75 are not significant (noise from small lineages), which is why R1 and R2 rank by selectivity only within the significant block. AUROC over the whole list and average precision are not used (most of the discrimination is "real dependency vs noise", and unlabelled pairs are not true negatives).

## 4. Minimum size

- The primary endpoint is computed only if the primary set has at least 15 eligible pairs from at least 10 distinct genes, **and** at least 8 positive controls from at least 8 distinct genes lie inside the q < 0.05 block. With 15–30 eligible pairs all results are labelled exploratory.
- Otherwise: only descriptive results (the per-control table); no method comparison. In that case the main evidence of the paper comes from plan A's simulation, and the manuscript does not depend on plan B.
- Expected outcome stated in advance: the list may be small and the primary comparison is likely to be "not distinguishable". This will not be presented as a headline in either direction, and the criteria will not be changed after the results are seen.
